OverviewModule checker

Check a battery module before you build it.

Choose the cell, the series and parallel count, busbar, cooling, balancing and BMS. BESS-Twin runs the duty cycle at 25 °C, on a 40 °C day and at end of life. It checks 12 requirements and 12 interface rules, and links each result to the bench test that would confirm it.

Module checker two designs, one mission, every requirement

Pick two designs, change the mission, and see temperature, energy, end of life, mass, cost, size and every rule check update. Same equations as bess_platform.py v3.0 (parity-tested).

1Mission and conditions

Default M1: 140 A for 20 s at the start of every 300 s, 60 A in between, until a limit trips

2Hot-cell temperature and module voltage through the mission

The hot-cell line is the module temperature plus the extra rise of the worst parallel cell. A run ends at the hot-cell limit, the 2.5 V per cell cutoff, or an empty cell. The dashed lines are the derate threshold and the limit.

3Requirement and rule checks

FAIL breaks a requirement or hard limit. MARGINAL passes with less than 3 K margin, which is inside model uncertainty. WARN is an interface risk that needs evidence.
